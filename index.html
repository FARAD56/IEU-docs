System administrator

Sees every region and school in the country. Adds directors and assigns each one to a region.

Director

Everything a director does applies to the schools in their own region: schools broken down by district, staff, students, approvals, reports and the audit log.

Head of school

Works inside one school: staff, classes and students, and approving teachers' requests and results. Assistant heads share the work.

Teacher

Sees only their own classes and subjects. A form teacher owns a class and sees all its marks; a subject teacher marks only what they teach.

Requests, not edits

Nothing important changes without a signature

Teachers don't edit student records directly. They raise a request; a head approves or rejects it, and every step stays in the request's history and the audit log.

The school year

Built around how a year actually runs

  1. Start of year

    Set up

    Academic year and terms, classes for each grade level, a form teacher for each class and a teacher for each subject.

  2. Every day

    Registers

    Teachers take each class's register in one go. Staff attendance is recorded too.

  3. Through the term

    Assessments

    Class work, tests and exams are scored against the class's approved grading scheme.

  4. End of term

    Results

    Totals, grades and positions are computed, submitted to the head, approved and locked.

  5. End of year

    Promotion

    Students move up under the school's promotion policy, including its minimum attendance, through approval requests.
