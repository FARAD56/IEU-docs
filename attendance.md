# Attendance

Attendance is kept for students and for staff.

- **Sessions.** A register is taken as an attendance session for a class on a given day.
- **Students.** Teachers mark the whole class in one go rather than student by student.
- **Staff.** Teachers' and heads' attendance is recorded in bulk too.
- **Reports.** Attendance reports summarize each class and school over a period.

Attendance isn't just a record. A student's attendance percentage for the year is checked against the school's promotion policy, which sets a minimum, when deciding who moves up. See [Academic structure]({% link academic-structure.md %}).

Staff leave goes through [approvals]({% link approvals.md %}) as a leave request.
